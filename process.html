How I work · Double Diamond, evolved

Talk. Learn.
Make. Repeat.

I use a Double Diamond process, but I do not worship the diagram. The goal is to turn messy signals into useful action.

Discover → Define → Design → Deliver or Develop

The principle behind the process

Equal empathy for the person using the product and the business making it possible.

01 · OPEN UP

Discover

I listen widely: users, clients, stakeholders, data, constraints, and the developer who knows where the edge cases live.

02 · GET PRECISE

Define

I turn research signals into a focused problem, shared priorities, and clear design or developer actions.

03 · MAKE OPTIONS

Design

I explore flows, interfaces, and prototypes while keeping user value and business value in the same conversation.

04 · MAKE IT REAL

Deliver
or Develop

I communicate clearly with engineers and increasingly build working prototypes myself using Codex and Claude Code.

The part diagrams miss

Client-facing without the theater

02

03

04
